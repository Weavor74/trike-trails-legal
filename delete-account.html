Delete your Trike Trails account

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Trike Trails and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon to open Profile.
  3. In the Account card, tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email triketrails.support@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject "Delete my Trike Trails account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email and password) and your profile (display name, home area, bio)
  • Your garage
  • Every route you published, and any reports filed against those routes
  • Reports you filed about other riders' routes

What is not deleted

  • Routes and tracks stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. See the privacy policy.
