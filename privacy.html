Trike Trails Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Trike Trails ("the app") helps riders of three-wheeled vehicles find, record and share routes. This policy explains what the app collects and what happens to it. Questions: triketrails.support@gmail.com.

Location

When you record a route or use navigation, the app reads your device location only while the app is open on screen. The app never asks for background location. Your live location is used on your device to draw your position and record a track. It is not sent to us. A recorded track leaves your phone only if you choose to publish it, and then it becomes a public route.

Maps

Map tiles are downloaded from USGS topographic map servers. Those servers receive normal web-request information such as your IP address and the tiles requested. Offline maps are stored on your phone and can be removed from each route's page.

Hosting and sharing

Account, profile, garage and published-route data are hosted by Supabase. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising SDKs. Data is sent over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, garage, published routes and reports tied to them. Details, including how to ask by email if you can no longer open the app, are on the account deletion page. Routes saved only on your phone are removed by uninstalling the app.

Retention

We keep account data until you delete your account. Moderation reports about a route are deleted with that route.

Children

Trike Trails is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
